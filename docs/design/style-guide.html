Palette

Every color is RGB555-representable. The channels were picked on the 0..31 grid and expanded the way the CGB does it, so a "white" is #E7EFDE and not #FFFFFF. The LCD gets four tones, the way a GBC background palette does. Signal colors are one per element, never mixed in gradients.

Why the project's Jersey 15 Charted is out

The repo loads Jersey 15 Charted. Opened in a font editor, a Charted glyph is one filled contour covering the whole em box with 46-unit holes on a 50-unit grid. The "chart" is the 4-unit grid lines. At 27px those lines are 0.08px wide, so the browser antialiases them into a faint gray haze across the entire glyph box, spaces included. You cannot see the grid and you lose contrast. The face only shows what it is at around 324px, where one cell is 12px and a line is 1px. Here it is at that size, next to the same numeral in plain Jersey 15.

Motion

There is no easing on a Game Boy Color. Frames change or they do not. Every transition here uses steps(). The blink is 533ms, roughly the 32-frame cadence of the text-box arrow. Buttons drop 2 dots in one frame. Stat bars fill in four steps. Under prefers-reduced-motion every duration becomes 0ms and the blink stops on the visible frame.

Scaling

The device does not stretch. At any width under 1280px the dot is 2px and the device is 352px of screen inside a 416px shell. At 1280px wide and 800px tall the dot becomes 3px and everything on the device grows by exactly 1.5x. Body copy stays 27px because 54px is a poster. The app can also set data-dot="2|3|4" on <html> from the SELECT button; 4 doubles the Jersey grid too.
